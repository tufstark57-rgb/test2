HOW WE WORK

Serious care.
Zero shortcuts.

01

Inspect

We assess paint, trim and interior condition before a product touches your car.

  • Paint depth and swirl assessment under dedicated LED inspection lighting
  • Panel-by-panel check for scratches, oxidation, water spots and fallout
  • Interior review across fabric, leather, plastics, vents and glass
  • You get a clear recommendation and a fixed quote before any work starts
02

Restore

Our detailers use certified 3M & Meguiar’s products matched to your car's needs.

  • pH-balanced snow foam pre-wash, then a safe two-bucket contact wash
  • Clay bar decontamination to lift embedded industrial fallout and tar
  • Machine polishing with certified 3M and Meguiar’s compounds to cut swirls
  • Interior deep clean: seat shampoo, steam, and trim and dashboard conditioning
03

Protect

We finish with durable protection so your investment stays immaculate for longer.

  • Ceramic or synthetic sealant applied in a controlled, dust-free bay
  • Hydrophobic top layer that sheds water, dust and Delhi road grime
  • Alloys, glass and exterior trim protected for one even, lasting finish
  • Aftercare guidance so the finish holds through summer heat and monsoon
